# Brand Guidelines

## Executive Summary
Northstar Health is a health startup serving Busy professionals aged 25-45 who want reliable, easy-to-use health services.. The brand should communicate reassuring, expert, calm qualities with a consistent identity across digital and print touchpoints.

## Brand Strategy
- **Purpose:** Why the brand exists: Make preventive care accessible through simple, human-centered digital services.
- **Vision:** Desired future state: Build the most trusted preventive health brand for busy urban professionals.
- **Mission:** Make preventive care accessible through simple, human-centered digital services.
- **Core Values:** trust, quality, simplicity

## Brand Identity
- **Logo Guidelines:** Use the logo consistently on light and dark backgrounds. Maintain clear space, avoid distortion, and keep a monochrome version available for restricted print use.
- **Color Palette:** #2563EB, #16A34A, #0F766E, #14B8A6, #E0F2FE, #F8FAFC, Preference note: calm green and soft blue
- **Typography:** Headings: clean sans-serif, Body: highly readable sans-serif, Preference note: clean modern sans serif
- **Imagery Style:** clean, bright, and human-centered

## Brand Communication
- **Voice:** reassuring, expert, calm
- **Tone:** professional
- **Tagline:** Care that fits your life.

## Brand Application
- **Website:** Use the brand palette, type hierarchy, and logo clear space rules on the homepage and product pages.
- **Social Media:** Keep captions, cover images, and story assets consistent with the selected tone and color system.
- **Marketing Materials:** Apply the same visual identity to brochures, pitch decks, ads, and email headers.
- **Presentation:** Use branded title slides, section dividers, and icon styling.

## Target Audience
Busy professionals aged 25-45 who want reliable, easy-to-use health services.

## Brand Personality
trustworthy, professional, reassuring

## Visual Identity Guidelines
Imagery should be clean, bright, and human-centered. The visual system should feel trustworthy, professional, reassuring and support the health context.

## Tone of Voice
The brand should sound professional. Keep copy aligned with the audience and the startup stage.

## Brand Positioning
Position the brand as credible, caring, and safe.

## Key Messaging
- What we do: Make preventive care accessible through simple, human-centered digital services.
- Who we serve: Busy professionals aged 25-45 who want reliable, easy-to-use health services.
- Why we matter: Build the most trusted preventive health brand for busy urban professionals.

## Usage Guidelines
- Always use the approved logo variants.
- Use the approved palette across digital and print.
- Keep spacing, typography, and tone consistent.
- Adapt tone slightly by channel, but keep the same brand personality.
