# Brand Guidelines

## Executive Summary
ScaleUP is a technology startup serving SMEs and growth-stage companies seeking AI implementation, automation, and data strategy support. The brand should communicate smart, clear, innovative qualities with a consistent identity across digital and print touchpoints.

## Brand Strategy
- **Purpose:** Why the brand exists: Deliver practical AI projects and consulting that help companies scale operations and decision quality.
- **Vision:** Desired future state: Become the trusted AI consulting partner for growth-stage companies in North Africa and Europe.
- **Mission:** Deliver practical AI projects and consulting that help companies scale operations and decision quality.
- **Core Values:** quality, trust, innovation

## Brand Identity
- **Logo Guidelines:** Use the logo consistently on light and dark backgrounds. Maintain clear space, avoid distortion, and keep a monochrome version available for restricted print use.
- **Color Palette:** #F5F5DC, #8B5A2B, #111827, #2563EB, #0EA5E9, #F8FAFC, Preference note: beige, brown
- **Typography:** Headings: modern sans-serif, Body: clean sans-serif, Preference note: clean modern sans-serif for headings and readable sans-serif for body
- **Imagery Style:** professional, minimalist, consultancy-oriented

## Brand Communication
- **Voice:** smart, clear, innovative
- **Tone:** professional
- **Tagline:** Scale smarter with AI.

## Brand Application
- **Website:** Use the brand palette, type hierarchy, and logo clear space rules on the homepage and product pages.
- **Social Media:** Keep captions, cover images, and story assets consistent with the selected tone and color system.
- **Marketing Materials:** Apply the same visual identity to brochures, pitch decks, ads, and email headers.
- **Presentation:** Use branded title slides, section dividers, and icon styling.

## Target Audience
SMEs and growth-stage companies seeking AI implementation, automation, and data strategy support.

## Brand Personality
professional, trustworthy, innovative

## Visual Identity Guidelines
Imagery should be professional, minimalist, consultancy-oriented. The visual system should feel professional, trustworthy, innovative and support the technology context.

## Tone of Voice
The brand should sound professional. Keep copy aligned with the audience and the startup stage.

## Brand Positioning
Position the brand as modern, reliable, and scalable.

## Key Messaging
- What we do: Deliver practical AI projects and consulting that help companies scale operations and decision quality.
- Who we serve: SMEs and growth-stage companies seeking AI implementation, automation, and data strategy support.
- Why we matter: Become the trusted AI consulting partner for growth-stage companies in North Africa and Europe.

## Usage Guidelines
- Always use the approved logo variants.
- Use the approved palette across digital and print.
- Keep spacing, typography, and tone consistent.
- Adapt tone slightly by channel, but keep the same brand personality.
- Review generated logo concepts with a designer before final lockup approval.
